HC2 WEB GAME - v0.9.1 (23 Oct) - KEEPER AI, TOP PRIORITY (Luca 14:56: "move gk ai dev to top priority we need to test it
+ include walking up etc when needed")
- Built on goalkeeping coaching (keeperstop.com positioning guide, FIFA's World Cup 2022 keeper analysis, FIFA's
  "defending the goal or the space" session). What he does, in order:
  ANGLE  on the line that halves the angle between the ball and his posts; ~2 m off his line for a ball 6 m away,
         ~5 m for one 25-30 m away.
  UP / SWEEPER  ball far away: he WALKS UP to the top of his box and beyond (~18 m behind his last defender, max 21 m;
         3 m more when his team has it in their final third). FIFA WC2022 average: 13.1 m - ours averages 11.6 m.
  SET    an attacker on the ball within 25 m: still, in his ready stance (PixelLab 'kset').
  RUSH / BLOCK  1v1 (a dribbler running at goal, nobody goal-side): comes out on the ball line, slows inside 10 m,
         stops ~5 m from him low and big.
  SMOTHER / SWEEP  a loose ball / through ball he reaches first (before the attacker, and his defenders can't):
         in the box he gathers it, outside he clears it with his feet.
  BACK   a lob over him while he's up: runs back.
  Speed  walks when nothing is on, jogs back into place, runs when they break; backs off facing the ball (the walk
         plays backwards).
- Back-pass rule (IFAB Law 12): a team-mate's pass / throw-in back to him = feet only. Under pressure he clears it long.
- Test Lab: new group GK (11 situations) + GK VIEW (FREE PLAY switch): the angle lines, a yellow diamond where he wants
  to be, a label with what he's doing and how far out.
- Checked: all 11 GK tests do what they should; the 40 keeper shot tests save about the same as before (25/40 vs 26/40,
  within the dice); 12 min CPU v CPU + 3-match bot soak: no crashes / stuck matches.

  in the final third, a shot or a ball into the box plays out; a corner or a free kick near goal is taken first; a
  penalty is always completed (the law); a goal in the last seconds gets its replay, then the whistle. In midfield he
  blows straight away. His patience: ~14 s.
- HALF TIME: two whistles, the players walk off to the tunnel, the camera goes to the stand, then the HALF TIME card
  (possession, shots, corners, fouls, cards). SUBS (shoot key): tap a player, then who comes on - 3 subs a match,
  fresh legs, no coming back. ENTER for the second half (or it starts by itself after 25 s). The computer may take off
  a tired man too. Everyone gets some energy back at the break.
- The demo behind the menu has added time too, but skips the sheet / walk-out / half-time card.
