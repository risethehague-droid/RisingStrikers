# Lane brief: Design & Vision

**Account:** the co-designer's. **Branch:** `friend/<topic>`. **Card lane:** Design & Vision.

## Scope
Overall game design and where HC2 goes next: themes, modes, settings, tone, progression, what's new to play. Also
the design questions on the board (rules, balance calls).

## Where things go
- `design/<topic>.md`: pitches, notes, decisions. One topic per file. Lead with the proposal in two lines.
- A decision Luca needs to make → a card in Design & Vision, kind *Decision*, with the options and your
  recommendation.
- An idea that needs code or art → a card in the right lane; the taskmaster writes the brief.

## A good pitch (one page)
Three directions, each with: the setting and tone, what's new to play, what it needs (art, code, rough size), one
risk. Mark your favourite. Put the link on the card and move it to Needs OK.

## Prototyping
You may prototype on your branch (code or art) and publish your own preview page. Your usual code files are
`game/src/ui.js` (menus, HUD, commentary) and `game/src/cup.js` (the cup mode); `game/src/README.md` lists the rest. Keep prototypes on the branch until
an OK'd card says to bring them in. Art rules still apply: PixelLab / Aseprite only.
