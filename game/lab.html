TAP KICK · new, Test Lab only
Tap SHOOT (let go within 0.15 s): a low, quick shot along your arrow that dies by about 15 m. Hold it: the power shot, as before.
ADD THE TAP KICK TO THE GAME? In matches it would change:
· SHOOT tapped (let go within 0.15 s) = the tap kick: about 65 km/h, low, straight along your arrow (or the way he faces), about 1° of error, dead by about 15 m. Held = the power shot, exactly as now.
· Set pieces, the chip, specials, volleys and headers: unchanged.
· The computer taps it in too: on the ball within 9 m of goal with the far corner out of the keeper's reach.
· Keepers: no change to how they save. In his reach he holds most taps; out of it they go in.
Nothing changes from this button. To add it: OK the t-010 card on the Taskboard (or tell the taskmaster "add the tap kick to the game"); the taskmaster switches TAP_KICK on in the code and publishes.
