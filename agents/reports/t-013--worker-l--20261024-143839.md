## What I did
- Added `GOAL_PHYS` (core.js): on in the Test Lab, off in matches, every number in `GP`.
- **The net has give.** It works as a spring with damping on the back, roof and sides, plus the outside side netting and the top of the roof.
  - The ball sinks in, slows down, is held a beat at the deepest point, then drops.
  - It's drawn by cutting the existing net art into 8 px cells that bend with the bulge and ripple. No new art.
- **Round posts and bar with real rebounds.**
  - The rebound depends on where on the tube the ball lands, with restitution and friction. The friction leaves spin, which grips on the bounce.
  - A square, hard hit gets a small lift, so it comes back at head height (GP4: over the six-yard line at 1.3 m).
  - No ping-pong.
- **Keeper pop-ups** in their own `gpPop(gk, kind, spd)`: parries up to 42%, guard breaks 45%. Halved on EASY for the computer's benefit.
- New Test Lab group GP1-GP8, and `GOAL_PHYS=1` for `tools/soak.js`.
