## What I did
- Charged headers / volleys: hold SHOOT as the ball comes, let go at the contact.
  - The hold is the power (0.8 s = full, like the ground shot).
  - Let go within 0.12 s before the contact = full power and on target.
  - Earlier = weaker and wider. Still holding at the contact = late (weaker and wilder).
  - Over 0.88 power = over the bar.
  - A tap = today's header / volley.
