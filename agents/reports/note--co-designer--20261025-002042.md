## Goal
The co-designer: "not necessarily making it harder, but making them smarter. Running, positioning, better passes,
less clunky. For both enemy teams and allied team."

A quick baseline on v0.11.0 (CPU v CPU, 10 match minutes, one seed):
- ground passes 129, kept 84%, to the man meant 78%
- through balls 5 (rare), kept 80%
- the computer's lobs 17, kept 41%, to the man meant only 12%
- soak (6 matches): ANIM_FLICKER 106 trips (players flashing between moves), 1 JITTER (8 turns in a second)
- known from Bug Watch (t-016): players stand still when someone is in their straight path

## What to do
1. Measure (new counters in tools/soak.js or a tools/aistudy.js):
   - running: time standing still / jogging on the spot in open play, sudden turns, runs that stop short,
     ANIM_FLICKER / JITTER by cause
   - positioning: team shape (width, depth, gaps between lines), team-mates bunching within 3 m, how many go to
     the ball, offering an angle to the man on the ball, running into space vs standing in it
   - passing: completion and "to the man meant" by type and distance, passes into a marked man, passes that a
     forward run was waiting for but never came, backwards passes when forward was on
   - your side vs their side: are your team-mates (AI with you as human) as sensible as the computer's team?
2. Watch: Bug Watch clips / tools/preview.js GIFs of the 5 clunkiest moments, each named by cause.
3. Plan: the top fixes ranked by how much they improve the look per effort; references in words only
   (how FC / PES / Sensible Soccer / Football Manager's match engine make players look smart: support angles,
   third-man runs, holding shape, pressing triggers, smooth turning and deceleration).
4. Decisions for Luca / the co-designer: what to fix first; anything that changes difficulty.
