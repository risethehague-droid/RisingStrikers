- Added the level-1 curving special pass. Hold SPECIAL, then press PASS / THROUGH / LOB with a bar in the bank. It plays cpass / cthru / clob (contact on frames 3 / 4 / 4) and spends ONE bar. With no bar you get the normal pass. While CURVE_PASS is on, SPECIAL alone fires the special shot on release, so the chord can be made.
- ball.js `curveStep`: sideways spin, on the grass and in the air, strongest early and wearing off.
