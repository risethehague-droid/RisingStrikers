## What I did
- **New option, LOB AIM: AUTO / RING.** It's in Pause > Options and on the start screen (a row under PACE). It's remembered per browser like PACE and defaults to AUTO. Every number is in `core.js` `LOB_AIM`.
- **AUTO (FIFA-style).** The lob goes to the team-mate who best fits your arrow.
  - Who counts: within 38° either side of the arrow and 8-45 m away. The cheapest wins: cost = his distance + an angle penalty + opponents near the drop. So a marked man loses to a free man further on.
  - It lands where his run takes him during the flight, plus a 1.2 m step.
  - Tap = the quick one. Hold = up to +35% height and +3 m on his run.
  - Nobody in the cone = into space along the arrow, 14-38 m by how long you held.
  - While you hold, the pass-aim marker on him is the only thing on screen.
- **RING.** The ring is still there, the arc is gone. Top speed 10 m/s (was 16), and its speed eases with the stick instead of jumping. The magnet is lighter and eases in: 35% pull instead of 60%, and no snap.
- **Camera (both).** An open-play lob no longer rides up. While a lob is in the air, the lead eases instead of jumping at the kick and the drop. In RING, the framing on the ring eases in after the press and hands back to the normal follow after the release. The t-007 ride-up for a keeper deflection stays.
