**1. Every restart gets a short SETUP phase:** the whistle / flag -> the players actually move into the restart's shape
(jog, not teleport: the ones nearby jog in, anyone too far away is moved under a quick TV-style wipe like the goal kick's,
never in plain sight) -> the taker walks up and places the ball -> then you aim / the computer takes it. Camera framing
the setup, a caption ("CORNER - IS", "OFFSIDE - FREE KICK IRO").
**2. Offside free kick:** the flag + whistle as now, then: attackers jog back onside and turn, the defending side
pushes up into its shape, the free-kick taker (a defender or the keeper if it's deep) places it at the spot; quick but
not instant.
**3. Corners:** the taker walks to the flag and places the ball in the quadrant; the attackers drift into the box,
markers pick them up goal-side, a man on the near post, the keeper sets; THEN the cross. No more instant teleport.
**4. Free kicks near goal / far, throw-ins, goal kicks:** the same idea at the right size - a wall forming on 9.15 m,
throw-in taker picking the ball up at the line, goal kick consistent with v0.10.4's.
**5. Pacing:** one table of setup times per restart kind, CLASSIC and ARCADE (ARCADE quicker). Starting points: throw-in
~1.5 s (ARCADE ~0.8), offside / foul free kick far out ~2.5 s (~1.5), free kick near goal with a wall ~4 s (~2.5),
corner ~4 s (~2.5). Your team's set piece: SHOOT / PASS / START skips to aiming once the shape is mostly in (never a
long wait you can't skip). Tune by playing it (and by the soak's time-in-play).

## How real football and other games do it (match this flow)
- Laws of the Game: free kicks are taken from where the offence happened (offside: where the player became involved);
  opponents 9.15 m away (5 m on throw-ins: 2 m from the taker); the ball is stationary. Real restarts take a while
  (corners often 20-40 s: players walking in, jostling, the taker placing the ball).
- FIFA / EA FC and PES / eFootball compress that into a few seconds: a camera cut to the taker placing the ball or
  walking to the flag, players visibly jogging into position, the wall shuffling into place, then control returns.
  Sensible Soccer-style arcade games are near-instant - that's what ARCADE can lean towards, but still never a
  teleport in view.
- Offside in TV football: the flag goes up, the whistle, the attacking team trots back, a defender (often the keeper or
  a centre-back) places it and plays it short or long - play restarts within seconds, smoothly.
