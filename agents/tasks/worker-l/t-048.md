## How real football and other games do it
- Real football: forwards chip a keeper who comes off his line, tap in rebounds and squared balls, and wingers take it
  to the byline before crossing. They are rarer than passes and shots, but every match has some.
- FIFA / PES: the AI mixes all three; a team that never does them looks robotic. Aim for "a few per match", not
  "every attack".
