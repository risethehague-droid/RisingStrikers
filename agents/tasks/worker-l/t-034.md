## How real football and other games do it
- Real football: aerial duels in the box at crosses and set pieces - two players rise together, the one who gets the
  first, firmer contact either heads it goalwards (a header on goal) or the defender heads it clear, high and wide.
  Bodies collide; the loser often ends up on the floor. Outside the box a header contest is just a knock-down.
- Captain Tsubasa / Inazuma Eleven: an in-the-box aerial clash becomes a mini-showdown - both leap, the screen freezes
  on the contact, the winner's header / volley flies at goal, the loser is sent flying.
- Button-mash minigames (Mario Party, Smash's grab escapes, FIFA's old shielding QTEs): each press visibly moves the
  meter by a fixed step and gives instant feedback (sound + shake); the meter never lies about who is winning.
