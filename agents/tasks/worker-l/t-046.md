## How real football and other games do it
- Real football: every match starts from nothing; nothing carries over from the last game but the table.
- FIFA / PES: a rematch or the next match of a tournament is a fresh kick-off; replays / "match seeds" in game QA are
  only useful when a match is fully determined by its set-up, which is what this fixes.
