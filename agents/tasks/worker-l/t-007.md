**1. Saves that read x / y / z.** The save plan picks the save from where the ball will cross (height and side) and how
hard it is:
- weak or medium, within reach: **catch** it (standing, chest or overhead; `catch` / `claim`)
- low to a side: **low dive** (`diveDown`, `diveL` / `diveR`); high to a side: **high dive** (`diveUp`)
- over his head but reachable: **jump** and catch it (a vertical leap: `diveUp` / `claim` at height)
- hard: **parry** it wide of the post, not back into the middle
- hard and high, or right under the bar: **tip it over the bar**
- a lob coming his way: once he has read it (PR #8's `gkRead`), **jump and catch** it if he gets there clean, **punch**
  it clear (`punch`) when it's crowded or too high to hold, BACK when it's going over him
- **Catch more when the kick is weaker:** the chance to hold grows as the ball's speed falls, by level (`dk`).

**2. More corners.** A keeper's touch now often sends the ball out: tipped over the bar, parried round the post,
fingertips to a lob, a hard shot he can only push away. The keeper's touch counts as the defending team's last touch,
so these become **corners**. Target over a 2 x 3 min match: about 4-8 corners (measure today first), with goals per
match within 25% of your base.

**3. Special saves that don't fully save it.** In `specials.js`, when the shot's special is stronger than his save (or a
strong normal shot beats a level-1 save), he gets a hand to it and it goes over or wide: a corner instead of a goal or a
clean save. Keep it fair: equal levels still save.

**4. The camera follows it.** When a save sends the ball up and out (or a lob goes up), the camera follows the ball until
it lands or goes out (`world.js`, the camera lines only).

**Animations:** use the keeper's existing frames (`catch`, `claim`, `diveDown`, `diveL`, `diveR`, `diveUp`, `punch`,
`pdive`, `pdiveH`, `kroar`). The taskmaster is making new PixelLab keeper moves tonight (a leap-and-grab for lobs, a
full-stretch side dive, a punch, a tip over the bar). Pick the animation through one small table (save type → animation
name, with a fallback), so the new ones drop in by name later: `kleap`, `kdivecatch`, `kpunch2`, `ktip`.
