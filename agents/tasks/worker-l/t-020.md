## Goal
Luca, 25 Oct 06:56: "arcade should have a smaller pitch, more rebounds that lead into aerials, more energy sp, make the
game feel incredibly intense in a dynamic sense". ARCADE PACE part 1 (t-004, v0.10.3) did movement and less waiting;
design/arcade-pace.md lists the rest of the dials.

All of this in ARCADE only - CLASSIC plays exactly as today (prove it with the soak).

**1. Rebounds:** in Arcade the ball doesn't go out for throw-ins, goal kicks or corners: it comes back off the
touchlines and bylines (invisible boards along the lines, outside the goal mouth), lively, often popping up - so loose
balls and aerial chances keep coming (headers, volleys, the aerial specials). No new art; nothing drawn.
**2. Aerials:** make the rebound balls worth attacking: players read and attack them (team AI), the aerial battle /
headers / volleys trigger from them as from crosses.
**3. More energy - SP:** the SP meter fills ~1.5-2x faster in Arcade, plus a little for winning an aerial, a rebound
volley, a big save. Specials a few times a match each side.
**4. Intensity:** design/arcade-pace.md's B, C, E dials that aren't in yet (ball a bit livelier, shots a bit harder,
shorter special cut scenes, hit-stop on shots / specials only, quick restarts after goals). Aim ~16 shots, ~4-5 goals
in 2 x 3 minutes. Say what you set.
**5. Smaller pitch - a plan, not built yet:** the stadium art is pre-projected (tools/stadium/cfg.py,
docs/lanes/stadiums-maps.md - the co-designer's lane). Find out whether tools/stadium/world_build.py can build a
smaller Arcade pitch (e.g. ~80 x 52 m) from the existing PixelLab tiles with no new drawing, and what code would have
to change (PITCH_L / PITCH_W are constants everywhere). Write it up in the PR (options, cost, what Luca / the
co-designer must decide). Don't change the pitch size in this task.
