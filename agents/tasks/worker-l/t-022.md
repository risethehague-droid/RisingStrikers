## Goal
Luca, 25 Oct 07:04 ("very important right now"): "headers and volleys can also be charged up and hit like a power shot
(from kicks) if timed properly ... make the animation feel impactful (add frames flashing sparks etc but still subtle
compared to special moves)".

Today a header / volley at a drop is a fixed strike (actions.js: SHOOT at a drop, t-008's receiving windows, the aerial
battle). Shots on the ground already charge (hold SHOOT = power, `charge`, `doShot`).

**1. Charge in the air:** hold SHOOT as the ball comes in; let go as it reaches you = the header / volley with that
power. Timing matters: release in a short sweet window around the contact moment = full power and accuracy ("timed
properly"); early / late = weaker and wilder. Too much power over the bar, like a ground shot. A plain tap still does
today's header / volley. Same for the computer by level (its timing is a dice by level, not perfect). Works with the
aerial battle (the winner's strike is charged by how long he's been holding).
**2. Impact, subtle:** on a well-timed one: a 2-3 frame white flash on the ball, a small spark burst from the existing
fx atlas, a tiny hit-stop and a light camera nudge. Clearly less than a special move (no banner, no cut-in). Nothing
drawn in code beyond the existing fx / tints.
**3. Animation:** the taskmaster is making new PixelLab header and volley moves from more angles (batch 20: names
like `header2-<dir>`, `volley2-<dir>`, diving header). If they're in the kits when you start, pick them by the ball's
angle and height; if not, keep the picks in ONE table so they drop in, and use today's frames.
**4. Keeper:** a charged header is a shot like any other for t-007 / t-017's save logic (pace sets hold / parry).
