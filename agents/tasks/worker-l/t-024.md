## Goal
Luca, 25 Oct 07:14: "OFF SIDE rule MANDATORY just make it smooth". Today the game has no offside.

**1. The rule (IFAB Law 11, simplified):** at the moment a team-mate plays the ball (pass, through ball, lob, cross,
shot that rebounds to him counts only if it came from a team-mate's touch), a player is in an offside POSITION if he's
in the opponents' half and nearer their goal line than both the ball and the second-last opponent (usually the last
outfield defender; the keeper counts). He's only penalised if he then becomes involved: receives / touches the ball
(or clearly blocks the keeper / a defender from it - optional, say if you skip it). Not offside: his own half, level
counts as onside, from a throw-in, a goal kick or a corner, a ball last played by an opponent (a deliberate pass /
clearance; a deflection or a save doesn't reset it). Snapshot positions at the kick's contact frame, not the button
press.
**2. Smooth:** the call is instant and short: the assistant referee's flag shown as a HUD callout ("OFFSIDE!" in the
style of the existing callouts, the flagged player marked a moment), a whistle (existing sound), play stops where he
was and the defending team takes a quick free kick from that spot (the existing free-kick set piece, the computer takes
it at once in Arcade). No long freeze, no replay. A Rami line or two.
**3. The AI plays to it:** attackers time their runs - they hang on the last defender's shoulder and go when the pass
comes (t-012's runs, through balls, lobs check the receiver is onside before choosing him); defenders hold a sensible
line (no deliberate offside trap yet). Your team-mates too. It must not turn matches into whistle-fests: aim for ~1-3
offsides a match.
**4. You:** when you're about to pass to someone offside, the pass-aim marker shows it (a small hint, e.g. the marker
tinted red), so it's never a surprise. In Options: OFFSIDE ON / OFF (default ON - "mandatory"; OFF only for testing).
