# t-045: the rare no-bars catch

**Today:** only a keeper whose side spends SP bars on a SPECIAL SAVE (`p.spSave`, ball.js ~1052-1062, SAVE_POWER,
`clash`) can answer a special shot. Without bars it always goes in.

**Luca's pick (the recommended option on card design-q-keeper):** he can still stop it, **rarely (~1 in 8)**, when he's
**set** (on his line / in position, not diving late, not stunned or cooling down) and **his guard holds** (the shot is
on target and within his reach, the same on-target test as `gkSpOnTarget`). Otherwise it goes in as now. A special
stays exciting but never certain.

## How real football and other games do it
- Inazuma Eleven: a keeper with no technique (no hissatsu) rarely stops a hissatsu shot, and it looks like a desperate
  block, not a clean catch. Mario Strikers: the keeper can still stop a Megastrike without a special of his own.
- Real football: a keeper who is set and square makes the "impossible" save sometimes; one caught moving doesn't.
- So: no cut-in, no SP spent, no aura. A strained parry or a spill (the ball pushed away, not held), with the crowd's
  "ooh" and Rami's surprised line if one fits (reuse an existing save line; don't add art).
