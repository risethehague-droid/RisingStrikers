## How real football and other games do it
- Real keepers catch "with soft hands", bring the ball into the chest as they land, get up holding it, then often walk
  it out a few steps before distributing (the Laws give them 6 seconds; they may move freely inside the box).
- FIFA / EA FC and PES: the ball is drawn locked to the keeper's gloves through the catch and landing; he can then run
  inside the box carrying it at chest height (a dedicated carry run), and distribute by throw, roll, drop-kick or
  dropping it to his feet. No ball ever floats next to his hands.
