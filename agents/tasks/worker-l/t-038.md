## How real football and other games do it
- FIFA / EA FC's **Practice Arena** and **Skill Games**, PES's **Training mode**: you land in a free practice pitch by
  default; every move list item can be tried on the spot; a clear menu (with search / categories) leads to drills.
- Fighting / sports games' training modes (Street Fighter, Smash, Rocket League free play): a move list with a button
  to "perform" each move, a dummy that reacts, and on-screen inputs. The playground is the front door; drills are a
  menu away.
