## How real football and other games do it
- TV football: after the ball goes out for a corner the director cuts away (a replay or a crowd shot) and comes back on
  the wide shot of the taker at the flag with the box filling - the "cut" hides the 20-40 s of walking.
- FIFA / EA FC and PES / eFootball: a brief fade or hard cut to the set-piece camera behind the taker; players are
  already near their spots when the picture comes back, then shuffle the last metres. The fade is short (well under a
  second) and skippable.
- Sensible Soccer: near-instant, no cut - fine for ARCADE's shorter timing.
