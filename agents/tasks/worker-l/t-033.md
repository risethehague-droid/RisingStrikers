## How real football and other games do it
- Real keepers: a low dive is a sideways collapse - step across, push off the near leg, land on the side (hip, then
  shoulder), arms reaching ahead, eyes on the ball. A high dive is a sideways spring, body roughly horizontal. Nobody's
  feet go straight up.
- FIFA / EA FC, PES: the dive animation travels sideways along the goal line, body horizontal to the ground in the
  camera's view; the ball locks into the hands at contact. Sensible Soccer: the keeper sprite lies flat sideways.
