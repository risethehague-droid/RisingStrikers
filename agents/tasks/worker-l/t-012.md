**1. Wing play** (the computer's attacks, and your computer team-mates' runs)
- Wide players keep the width and carry the ball down the flank when the middle is crowded; a full-back overlaps.
- From wide in the final third, the attacker looks for a **cross or lofted ball into the box** (the open-play lob from
  v0.10.3, which now really reaches its height) aimed at a runner, not at the keeper.
- **Runs to meet it:** a near-post run, a far-post run, someone arriving at the edge of the box for a knock-down.
  When you're the one wide, your computer team-mates make those runs too.
- The man who meets it **goes for the aerial shot**: header, volley, overhead (`actions.js` headers / volleys) and,
  with SP in the bank, **the aerial specials** (AIR BLAZE / SKY THUNDER / PHOENIX in `specials.js`).
- Still football: the computer keeps a mix of play (through the middle, shots from distance, wing play), and the
  levels stay apart (Easy crosses less and worse, Rise Warrior picks the right moment).

**2. Find exploits and fix them.** Run many simulated matches and look for anything that repeats too cleanly: the same
cross always scoring, the back post always open, the keeper never coming, a cross-out-corner loop, attackers waiting
forever wide, an aerial special that can't be saved, your team-mates standing still. Fix what you find and list it.

Targets for a 2 x 3 min match (measure today first): crosses / lofted balls into the box about 8-14 (both teams),
aerial shots (headers, volleys, aerial specials) about 3-6, goals not more than 25% above today.
