## How real football and other games do it
- Match stats (FIFA / PES / Opta): a "shot" is any attempt at goal with any legal body part, on target or not; "on
  target" is the subset that would go in without the keeper / a defender. Blocked shots count as shots.
- Every football game resets the match state on kick-off of a new match (clock, cards, stamina, meters) - a rematch is a
  clean slate.
