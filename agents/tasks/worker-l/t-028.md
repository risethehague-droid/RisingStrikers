## How real football and other games do it (match this feel)
- Inazuma Eleven: a hissatsu shot against a hissatsu catch - the ball grinds against the technique for a moment, power
  numbers decide, and the catch either holds, is deflected, or shatters and the keeper is knocked into the goal.
- Captain Tsubasa: the keeper gets both hands to a super shot and is driven back over the line with it (the "body"
  outcome); a weaker shot is caught cleanly and he roars.
- Shaolin Soccer / Mario Strikers (Megastrike): the keeper can reach anything during the special, but a strong enough
  shot carries him into the net. Real football has no specials, but the readable part is real: a keeper who gets a
  strong hand to a shot pushes it wide or back out (a parry into a dangerous area is a real mistake keepers make).
- Keep it short: a clash that holds play for more than ~1 s every time gets tiresome (Inazuma lets you skip). ENTER
  should cut it short, like the cut scene.
