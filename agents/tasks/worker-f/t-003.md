## What to build
- **A mode setting** (court rectangle, goal width and height, players a side, no keepers, short halves) whose
  defaults are today's 11 v 11. Today's match must play exactly as before: prove it with the same seeded matches
  (the split check's method, or `tools/soak.js` repro lines) giving the same scores on `main` and your branch.
- **The court:** for now a 28 x 15 m area of today's pitch, centred, with walls nobody can see. Mark the corners
  and the small goals only with art that already exists (corner flags, goal sprites scaled down) or none: CLAUDE.md
  "Art", nothing drawn in code. The camera and the radar stay on the court.
- **The walls:** the ball rebounds off the boards (a sensible restitution, some spin and speed lost, a sound from
  the existing effects). Players can't leave the court. Wall passes and one-twos off the boards should work for the
  computer too: give the AI a simple "play it off the wall" option.
- **The rules:** no keepers and no hands; a goal needs the whole ball over the line inside the small goal; kick-off
  restarts straight away after a goal; no throw-ins, corners or goal kicks; fouls are free kicks with the wall
  rules; nothing else from the 11-a-side flow (no offside; skip the walk-out, the coin toss and half-time subs).
- **5 v 5 shape and AI:** a 2-2 or 1-2-1 shape scaled to the court, pressing and support that make sense in a small
  space. Stamina and specials as today.
- **Entry:** a `STREET 5s ▶` button on the start screen that plays you v the computer at the chosen level. WATCH
  mode works in it too (`watch.js`).
- **Bug Watch** knows the court: OUT_LIVE, GOAL_MISSED, OFF_WORLD and the like check the court, not the big pitch.
- **Simulations:** add a `mode=street` option to `tools/soak.js` so the nightly QA can play STREET 5s matches too.
