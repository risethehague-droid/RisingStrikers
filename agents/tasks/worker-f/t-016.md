## Goal
Two bugs Luca marked in Bug Watch (v0.8.9 clips, USCS v LUC / USCS v THUAS, minute 8). He OK'd them as a worker task
(card qa-20261024-3, 24 Oct). His notes, as written:

1. Keeper: "if ball is close enough, the keeper should not give his back to the ball". The keeper turned away from a
   loose ball a few metres from him (a turn toward his post / position while the ball was right there).
2. Outfield: "If player path is blocked they should find alternative paths to the ball or to be useful." THUAS #3
   stood still because a team-mate / opponent was in the straight line to his target.

**First check they still happen on v0.11.0.** The keeper AI was rebuilt since (t-007: saves by x / y / z, holds,
tips) and the wing play is new (t-012). If one no longer happens, say so with the proof (the Test Lab test below)
and skip its fix.

**1. Keeper facing.** When the ball is loose (no owner) or with an attacker, and within ~6 m of the keeper, he
faces the ball whatever he's doing: shuffling back to his line, side-stepping, setting. He may move backwards or
sideways toward his spot (backpedal / side-step with the frames that exist), but never shows his back to it.
Moving away with the ball far off stays as it is.

**2. Blocked paths.** A player whose straight line to his target (the ball, a pass, his position, a run) is blocked
by another player steps around: a small sideways offset that clears the blocker, chosen to the side with more space,
then back on line. No standing still for more than ~0.5 s with a target in front of him. Keep it cheap (no full
path-finding) and keep the team shape.
